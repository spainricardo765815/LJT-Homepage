# Junteng Liu - Personal Homepage

Personal academic homepage of Junteng Liu, a first-year PhD candidate at the HKUST NLP Group, Hong Kong University of Science and Technology.

Built with the [Academic Pages](https://github.com/academicpages/academicpages.github.io) Jekyll template.

## Site structure

- `_pages/about.md` - front page (about section): biography, research interests, education, research experience, honors & awards, publications, and contact information
- `_pages/cv.md` - curriculum vitae: education, research experience, skills, honors & awards, and publications
- `_pages/publications.html` - publications page, generated from the `_publications/` collection
- `_config.yml` - site-wide settings and author sidebar information
- `_data/navigation.yml` - header navigation
