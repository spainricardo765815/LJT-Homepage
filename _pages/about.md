Biography
======

Junteng Liu is a first-year PhD candidate at the HKUST NLP Group, Hong Kong University of Science and Technology, supervised by Professor Junxian He. He received his B.Eng. from Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU) in June 2024, where he was previously advised by Professor Junxian He during his undergraduate studies. His research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning.

Research Interests
======
* LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning
* Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM)
* LLM Truthfulness and Interpretability
